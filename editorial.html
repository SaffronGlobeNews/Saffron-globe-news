Editorial Policy

Our approach to sourcing, attribution and automated news presentation.

Source attribution

Stories presented from external publishers retain a link to the original report. Readers should consult the original publisher for the complete article, quotations, corrections and subsequent updates.

Reporting and context

Saffron Globe News aims to distinguish reported facts, attributed statements and contextual explanation. Headlines and summaries should not imply original reporting when the underlying information comes from another publisher.

Automated feed

The automated news desk can display stories supplied by external feeds. Automated summaries are intended to point readers toward the original report and may be changed or removed when the source feed changes.

Corrections

If you identify a factual or attribution problem, please use the repository's public project contact channel to report the specific page and issue.
