Privacy Policy

How Saffron Globe News handles information on this website.

Information collected

Saffron Globe News currently does not require an account. The website may store limited preferences in your browser, such as the selected light/dark theme and local article-read counts. These local values are stored on your device and are not sent to Saffron Globe News.

External services

The site uses externally hosted fonts, images, news-source pages and video embeds. Those third-party services may process requests according to their own privacy policies.

Analytics

Saffron Globe News uses Google Analytics 4 (GA4) to measure website usage, such as page views and general engagement. The GA4 measurement ID is used to send analytics events to Google Analytics. Analytics data may be processed by Google according to Google's applicable policies and settings.

Newsletter

The newsletter interface on the homepage is currently a presentation feature and does not by itself create a mailing-list account or send your email to a newsletter provider.

Changes

This page may be updated when the site's services or data practices change.
